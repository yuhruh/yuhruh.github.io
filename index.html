05About

I'm a Ruby on Rails developer based in Taiwan with a background in chemistry. Most of my side projects start with a spreadsheet at home that has outgrown itself: my family's household bills became Household Tracker, and my cats' feeding log became Pet Feeding Tracker.

I like deconstructing problems, writing down the root cause before the fix, and shipping in small, reviewable steps. I also contribute to the Rails ecosystem, mostly fixes and zh-TW translations.

Away from the keyboard I read up on cat behavior 😼, do core workouts 💪 and practice yoga.
